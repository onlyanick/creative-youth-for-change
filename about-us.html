Creative Youth for Change

A forward-thinking youth-led organization aimed toward empowering young individuals around the world. We nurture young people and enable them to work toward a prosperous and just future for everyone.

Our Mission

Education is not a privilege, it is a fundamental right.

Our mission is to empower tomorrow's leaders and equip them with the necessary skills and qualities to contribute towards brighter futures. We aim to rear the next generation of change-makers and leaders.

Our primary mission is to create an inclusive, welcoming, and supporting environment where young individuals from diverse backgrounds can come together, share different perspectives, celebrate diversity, and work towards common goals. Additionally, our mission aligns with the United Nation Sustainable Development Goals.

Our Vision

Empowering youth today to build a better world tomorrow.

We strive to be the leading youth-led organization and amplify youths to be the agents of change in their communities. We believe in the potential of young people and we are certain that they can bring revolutions and positive changes.

We are driven by a vision of a world where all young persons have the opportunity to thrive and contribute meaningfully towards the global society.

Our Commitment to the SDGs

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a collection of 17 interlinked global goals designed to be a "greenprint to achieve a better and more sustainable future for all." At Creative Youth for Change, we align our mission and programs with these goals to create meaningful impact.

Our work centers on five SDGs: Quality Education (SDG 4), Gender Equality (SDG 5), Reduced Inequalities (SDG 10), Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions (SDG 16), and Partnerships for the Goals (SDG 17). Together with our additional areas of work, they guide us toward a more equitable and sustainable world.

Quality Education (SDG 4)

We work to ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and to promote lifelong learning opportunities for all. Through our Education as a Right program , we provide free online classes and language learning opportunities for students worldwide, preparing youth for the challenges of tomorrow.

Gender Equality (SDG 5)

We strive to achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls. Through our She Leads program we empower women and girls to become leaders in their communities, and we advocate for equal rights and opportunities for all genders, especially in marginalized communities.

Reduced Inequalities (SDG 10)

We aim to reduce inequality within and among countries. We focus on underrepresented and marginalized communities, ensuring equal access to opportunities.

Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions (SDG 16)

We promote peaceful and inclusive societies for sustainable development. Our Humanity Matters program addresses global challenges and promotes peacebuilding through dialogue, fostering understanding to build peaceful, just societies worldwide. We also work to protect and promote fundamental human rights and dignity for all people.

Partnerships for the Goals (SDG 17)

We strengthen the means of implementation and revitalize the global partnership for sustainable development. We collaborate with local and global organizations to amplify our impact and reach more communities, while developing leadership skills and empowering young people to become agents of change in their communities.

Child Protection

We ensure safe environments and opportunities for children to learn and grow. Every child has the right to be protected from harm, abuse, exploitation, and neglect, and to grow up with dignity, respect, and the freedom to reach their full potential.
