Education as a right

Education is not a privilege. It's a fundamental right.

Our first and primary project, launched in November 2023, with the mission to provide quality education for students worldwide, with a specific focus on underrepresented and underprivileged communities.

Our main goal is to make education accessible to everyone, mainly to marginalized girls and women in Afghanistan who have been deprived of learning.
