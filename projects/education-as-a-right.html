Education as a right

Education is not a privilege. It's a fundamental right.

Our first and primary project, launched in November 2023, with the mission to provide quality education for students worldwide, with a specific focus on underrepresented and underprivileged communities.

Our main goal is to make education accessible to everyone, mainly to marginalized girls and women in Afghanistan who have been deprived of learning.

About the project

The Education as a Right Project (EaaRP) is a youth-led educational initiative established in August, 2022 to expand access to learning opportunities for students who face barriers to education, particularly young people from marginalized communities in developing countries. A central focus of EaaRP is supporting Afghan girls who have been excluded from formal schooling and academic life, while also providing educational opportunities to marginalized learners across different countries.

The project has established programs including a University Mentorship Program and a Language Academy, which has offered courses in English, Urdu, Chinese, French, Turkish, and Arabic. Building on this experience, EaaRP now seeks to expand its educational offerings to include STEM courses, leadership and self-development programs, art and design, additional language classes, and preparation for internationally recognized examinations such as TOEFL, IELTS, DET, and SAT. To make this expansion possible and increase the project's reach and effectiveness, EaaRP is seeking funding and other forms of support from suitable partners and funding organizations.

Our Vision

Education provides a foundation for individual development, social participation, economic opportunity, and long-term community development. Yet access to meaningful educational opportunities remains unequal, particularly for young people living in marginalized or challenging circumstances.

Barriers may include limited access to educational institutions, financial constraints, displacement, social restrictions, language barriers, inadequate academic guidance, and limited access to specialized learning opportunities.

For Afghan girls who have been excluded from formal education and academic life, these challenges are particularly significant. When conventional educational pathways become inaccessible, alternative opportunities for continued learning and development become essential.

EaaRP was established to respond to these challenges by creating accessible educational opportunities, academic mentorship, and learning pathways for young people who may otherwise have limited access to them.

Our Mission

The project aims to expand access to inclusive and meaningful educational opportunities for marginalized young people, enabling them to continue learning, develop relevant skills, pursue higher education, and strengthen their capacity to participate in their communities and future opportunities.

This mission is carried out through some objectives:

Access to education

Education as a Right works to expand access to educational opportunities for marginalized students and learners facing barriers to conventional education. The project provides academic mentorship and guidance for students pursuing higher education, while placing particular attention on Afghan girls who have been excluded from formal academic life.

Leadership and self-development

The project creates opportunities for young people to develop the skills, confidence, and knowledge needed to engage actively in their communities and pursue their goals. Through upcoming STEM, leadership, self-development, and art and design programs, it will strengthen interpersonal, creative, and practical skills alongside academic development.

Language education

EaaRP supports language learning as an essential part of educational access and personal development. By improving opportunities for language education, the project helps learners strengthen their academic communication skills and have access to more educational and professional opportunities.

Our Projects

University Mentorship Program

The University Mentorship Program provides students with guidance and support related to higher education and academic pathways. The program helps learners better understand their educational options and make informed decisions regarding their academic futures.

Language Academy

The Language Academy provides language-learning opportunities designed to strengthen communication skills and broaden access to educational and professional opportunities. Languages offered include: English, Urdu, Chinese, French, Turkish and Arabic.
