Humanity Matters

Understanding is where change begins.

A 2-week online debate program focusing on human rights, peacebuilding, equality, freedom, and democracy with a specific focus on Afghanistan, Palestine, and Ukraine crises.

About the project

Humanity Matters is a two-week online debate and summit program established by Creative Youth for Change, the parent organization of the Education as a Right Project (EaaRP), to bring together human rights advocates, gender equality activists, peacebuilders, ambassadors, and youth change-makers under the theme "Humanity, Peacebuilding, Equality, Freedom, and Democracy."

Set to launch as an annual program starting in 2027, Humanity Matters will convene student participants and young change-makers alongside UN officials, ambassadors, and established advocates to engage directly with the world's most pressing human rights challenges, with a particular focus on the crises in Afghanistan, Palestine, and Ukraine.

To make this vision possible and expand its reach and impact, Humanity Matters is seeking funding and partnerships with suitable organizations and supporters.

Our Vision

Real, lasting change begins with honest dialogue and mutual understanding. Yet across the globe, communities facing conflict, displacement, and inequality often find their voices absent from the platforms where decisions about their futures are made.

Barriers to meaningful global dialogue may include restricted access to international platforms, limited representation for affected communities, few opportunities for young advocates to engage directly with policymakers, and a shortage of spaces where activists, officials, and everyday people can speak candidly about complex crises.

Humanity Matters was established to respond to these challenges by creating a platform for open discussion, mutual understanding, and collaborative advocacy among human rights defenders, peacebuilders, and global leaders. We believe that discussion and understanding are essential first steps toward making real, lasting change.
